Scoring System & EV Formula

Players submit an exact scoreline before each match. Points are awarded as follows.

Point Structure

Exact scoreline 3 pts
Correct direction (W/D/L)
wrong score 1 pt
Wrong direction 0 pts

Expected-Value Formula

EV(ga–gb) = pdir + 2 × pexact

This follows directly from the scoring rule: 1 pt if direction is correct, plus an extra 2 pts if exact.

pdir = phome if ga > gb,  pdraw if ga = gb,  paway if ga < gb
pexact = P(GA=ga | λa) × P(GB=gb | λb)
GA, GB ~ Poisson(λa), Poisson(λb) independent

Structural Bias Against Draws

The direction bonus term pdir always punishes draw predictions because pdraw ≈ 25–30% sits well below phome win ≈ 40–50% in most matches. Even when 1–1 and 1–0 share identical pexact, the 1–0 prediction dominates on EV: its direction bonus is ≈15–20pp higher. A draw scoreline must compensate with a substantially higher pexact, which only occurs at very low λ.

The Boundary Region

Symmetric case (λa = λb = λ). The chart plots two curves: the minimum pdraw required for a draw scoreline to be best EV, and the pdraw that symmetric Poisson actually implies.

Boundary Condition

pdraw > ⅓ + (4/3) × [P(bestwin) − P(bestdraw)]
λ ≤ 1: bestdraw = 0–0  (e−2λ),  bestwin = 1–0  (λ e−2λ)
λ > 1: bestdraw = 1–1  (λ²e−2λ),  bestwin = 1–0 or 2–1
Crossover: λ ≈ 0.95

Interpretation

The draw-optimal regime (shaded green) is narrow. Both conditions must hold simultaneously: the xG rates must be low enough that draw scorelines have higher pexact relative to win scorelines, and the match must genuinely be likely to be a draw.

In practice, pdraw rarely exceeds 0.33 in Poisson models unless both λ < 1.0 — but then the Poisson-implied pdraw curve barely crosses the boundary threshold, making the feasible region extremely thin.

The Knockout Calibration Puzzle

The model was trained without a stage feature. Systematic miscalibration emerges between group-stage and knockout matches — and the direction of the error is opposite in each.

Draw-Rate Calibration

Stage Actual draw rate Predicted pdraw Error
Group stage 24.3% 27.7% +3.4pp overpredicts
Knockout (at 90 min) 32.5% 28.4% −4.1pp underpredicts

Why the Gap?

Knockout matches induce tactical caution — the fear of elimination shifts teams toward defensive structures, compressing scoring rates. This effect is not captured by team strength features alone. The model therefore underestimates low-scoring outcomes in knockouts and overestimates them in group stage where teams attack more freely.

The Extra-Time Deduction

WC KO matches going to ET 35.8%
Avg goals at 90 min (ET matches) 1.83
Implied λ per team ≈ 0.92

λ ≈ 0.92 is right at the crossover threshold (0.95). In this regime, draw scorelines — especially 0–0 — become competitive with win scorelines on EV, and the model's underestimated pdraw makes them undervalued picks.

Implication: for knockout matches involving two comparable teams, consider checking whether the model's pdraw is systematically 4pp below the historical base rate. If so, a 0–0 prediction may have higher true EV than the model reports.
