How the numbers are calculated: Goal counts are modelled as independent Poisson distributions — the standard assumption in football analytics. Given the two expected-goal numbers you set, the tool computes the probability of every scoreline up to 12–12, then weights each one by the points it would earn (3 if exact, 1 if the right result, 0 otherwise) to get the average points ("expected points") per prediction.
